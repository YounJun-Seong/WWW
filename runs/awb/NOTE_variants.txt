CAVEAT — docs-only is NOT an evidence-only analogue of our EV message: AWB content agents attach validated documents to
only ~9% of their replies (full run: 9 of 98 finished content-agent sessions; docs run: 10 of 119); the rest carry only a
summary. Stripping the summary therefore removes nearly all transmitted information, so docs-only measures information
deprivation. What the variants do show: in AWB the transmitted content is overwhelmingly the content agent's claim
(summary); documents rarely reach the user agent (seen documents returned in 2/53 questions in `full`), and removing
documents changes nothing (summary-only = full). n = 53 and 6 discordant questions: diagnostic only.
